**The Rise of Vertical Farming**

Vertical farming, also known as indoor agriculture, has been gaining traction in recent years as a sustainable alternative to traditional farming methods. By growing crops in vertically stacked layers, often in controlled environments, vertical farms can produce up to 30 times more yield per acre than traditional farms. This approach not only reduces land use and water consumption but also minimizes the use of chemical pesticides and fertilizers.
