**The Challenge of Water Management**

Vertical farming presents a unique set of challenges when it comes to water management. With crops grown in vertically stacked layers, precise control over water distribution is crucial to prevent overwatering, which can lead to reduced yields, increased energy consumption, and higher operational costs. Traditional methods of water management often rely on manual intervention, leading to inconsistent results and wasted resources.
